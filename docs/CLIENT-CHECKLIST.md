# Seattle Door to Door Limo: launch checklist for the client

The new site is built. Before it goes live we need the answers below. Every claim on the site was carried over from the old site or written conservatively, but these items need the owner's confirmation.

## 1. Contact and bookings (launch blockers)

- **Reservations email:** does `reservations@seattledoortodoorlimo.com` exist and is it monitored, ideally 24/7? If not, which address should we use?
- **Where should booking requests go?** GoHighLevel, another CRM, Zapier, or email via a form service. Until this is set, the booking and contact forms end with "text, email or call us this request" instead of sending it automatically.
- **Photos:** the new site currently shows the photos from the old site. We will copy them into the new site before launch (with your OK), because the old files disappear when the domain switches over.

## 2. Reviews and numbers

- Current **Google, Yelp and Facebook** ratings and review counts, plus the profile links. The site shows 4.9 from 800+ overall; Google 4.9/250+, Yelp 4.8/300+, Facebook 4.9/250+.
- Are the four testimonials (Sarah Johnson, Michael Chen, Emily Rodriguez, David Thompson) real customers, quoted with permission and in their original wording?
- Can we keep **"100% on-time"** and **"1,000+ trips completed"**? If they can't be backed up, we'll drop them.
- **History:** should we lead with "since 2008" or "20+ years of experience"?

## 3. Fleet

- **Photos** of the real vehicles, especially the stretch limousine, Party Sprinter, Tesla and Escalade, ideally professional shots.
- For each vehicle:
  - seats and luggage capacity
  - how many of each the company has
  - which ones have Wi-Fi, charging, a partition, a bar or an entertainment system
- Is the Party Sprinter a Sprinter or a party bus? Does it seat 20, and does it have a dance floor?
- Is the Tesla Model S still in service?

## 4. Service area and airports

- Is **Paine Field** served as standard? Is **Boeing Field** (including private/FBO pickups) standard, on request, or not served?
- Is the service area just the six cities (Seattle, Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Tacoma, Everett), or all of King, Pierce and Snohomish counties?
- Are these SeaTac drive times right? Seattle 25, Bellevue 25, Kirkland 35, Redmond 35, Tacoma 30, Everett 45 min.
- Is there a public street address for Google? It must match the Google Business Profile.

## 5. Operations and policies

- Is every booking confirmed by a real person, 24/7, and how quickly? Is the phone answered around the clock, and are text replies offered?
- **Airport:** is meet and greet with a name sign included? How much free wait time is given (domestic and international)? Is flight tracking included on every booking?
- **Payments:**
  - Is anything charged at booking?
  - Which payment methods are accepted?
  - How are gratuity, tolls, parking and airport fees handled?
  - What are the cancellation and no-show terms?
  - Is there an hourly minimum?
  - Are extra stops charged?
- **Extras:**
  - Child seats (which types, and is there a fee)?
  - Champagne (is it supplied by the company, and is that compliant with WA rules)?
  - Red carpet and decorated vehicles?
  - Do prom and homecoming packages exist (alcohol-free policy, parent as booking contact)?
- Are corporate accounts with consolidated invoicing offered?
- Are all chauffeurs licensed, background-checked and trained? Is every vehicle detailed before each ride?

## 6. Content and legal

- Approve the two new Journal articles (SeaTac pickup guide; Pier 66 vs Pier 91 cruise transfers). They stay hidden until approved.
- Original publish date of "Why Black Car Service Is More Than Just a Ride".
- Have their lawyer review the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms**. Both are templates.
- Social media links (Instagram, Facebook) for the footer.
